### 1.4 Evicted content

File content evicted from a request to meet a budget (and any content whose slot's overflow form is
an entry, [§3.1](../3-core-algorithms/3.1-working-set-composition/index.md)) is named in that request, at the version it had, and can be brought back on demand.
Where replay cannot reproduce the content, the stored bytes are what the entry points to. The entry
is a recognition entry the model can expand (ARCHITECTURE.md "Spine entries are the recognition
surface").
