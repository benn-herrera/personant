### 8.1 Migration (format 2 → 3)

- **Registry.** Add `{From: 2, To: 3}` to `homeFormatMigrations`
  (`internal/memops/homeformat.go:44`) and set `version.CurrentHomeFormat = 3`
  (`internal/version/version.go:40`). The step is a restamp only.
- **Legacy sidecar.** Nothing reads `files.json`. The first B1c on the migrated home removes it
  ([§5.3](../5-store-format-and-retention/5.3-retention.md)); no step rewrites bytes before
  `Reconcile`.
- **Verify.** Verify never reads `files.json`, so a legacy sidecar on a format-3 home before its
  first barrier is not a finding.
